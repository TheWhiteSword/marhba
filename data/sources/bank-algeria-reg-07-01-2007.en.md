# Regulation No. 07-01 of 09/01/2007

Regulation No. 07-01 of 09/01/2007, relating to the rules applicable to current transactions with abroad and foreign currency accounts, amended and replaced Regulation No. 91-12 relating to the bank domiciliation of imports, Regulation No. 91-13 relating to the domiciliation of non-hydrocarbon exports, and Regulation No. 95-07 relating to exchange control.

The purpose of this regulation is to define the principle of convertibility of the national currency for current international transactions and the rules applicable to transfers to and from abroad related to these transactions, as well as the rights and obligations of foreign trade operators and authorized intermediaries in this matter.

The present document is intended to comment on the new measures introduced by this new regulation, the application of which falls directly on the customs administration.

## 6 - Operations carried out by travelers

### 6-1 Import and export of means of payment

Constitute means of payment within the meaning of Article 18 of the said regulation:

- banknotes;
- traveler's checks;
- bank or postal checks;
- the letter of credit;
- commercial paper;
- any other means or instrument of payment denominated in freely convertible foreign currency, whatever the medium used.

### 6-2 Import of means of payment (Article 19)

Within the meaning of Article 19, any traveler entering Algeria is authorized to import foreign banknotes and traveler's checks, subject to a customs declaration for any amount exceeding a threshold set by instruction of the Bank of Algeria.

To this end, and pending the publication of the instruction setting the threshold from which the traveler must submit a currency declaration, the service must require entering travelers to the national territory to submit the said declaration.

### 6-3 Export of means of payment (Article 20)

Any traveler leaving Algeria is authorized to export any amount in foreign banknotes and traveler's checks, up to:

- for non-residents: the amount declared on entry less sums regularly transferred to authorized intermediaries and to the exchange office;

- for residents: withdrawals made from foreign currency accounts within the limit of the ceiling set by instruction of the Bank of Algeria and/or amounts covered by an exchange authorization.

### 6-4 Import and export of national currency

Article 6 of the regulation introduced a new measure allowing travelers to import and export fiduciary currency in dinars, within the limit of a threshold to be set by instruction of the Bank of Algeria. Also, pending the setting of this threshold, our services must prohibit any import or export of Dinars.

Such are the clarifications deemed useful to communicate to you. Any difficulties encountered in the application of this regulation must be reported to me under this stamp.

## 1 - General principles

### 1-1 Notion of resident

Within the meaning of the provisions of Article 2 of Regulation No. 07-01, the following are considered:

- residents in Algeria: natural and legal persons who have the principal center of their economic activities there;

- non-residents: natural and legal persons whose principal center of economic activities is located outside Algeria.

### 1-3 Import and export of securities denominated in national currency

In accordance with Article 6, the import or export of any debt security, movable security or means of payment denominated in national currency is prohibited, except with the authorization of the Bank of Algeria.

Also, it is up to your services, during the control of travelers, to ensure that securities, in particular shares and bonds issued by public or private companies, Treasury Bonds and Cash Bonds, may only be imported or exported under cover of an authorization from the Bank of Algeria.
