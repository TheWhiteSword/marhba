# Currency Declaration

## Legal and regulatory basis

- Article 198 bis of the Customs Code;

- Article 72 of the Finance Law for 2016 and the circular issued in its application;

- Article 119 of the Finance Law for the year 2020, amending and supplementing Article 72 of the Finance Law for 2016;

- Ordinance 96-22 of 09/07/1996 relating to the repression of offenses against the legislation and regulations on exchange and capital movements to and from abroad, as amended and supplemented by Ordinance No. 03-01 of 19/02/2003 and by Ordinance No. 10-03 of 26/08/2010 (Articles 1, 1 bis and 2);

- Executive Decree No. 12-279 of 09 July 2012, setting the terms of organization and operation of the national file of offenders in matters of offenses against the legislation and regulations on exchange and capital movements to and from abroad (Articles 2, 3, 4 and 5);

- Bank of Algeria Regulation No. 16-02 of 21/04/2016 setting the declaration threshold for the import and export of banknotes and/or negotiable instruments denominated in freely convertible foreign currencies, by residents and non-residents;

- Instruction No. 04-2016 of 1 September 2016 relating to the export and import of Algerian banknotes.

The currency declaration is mandatory, on entry and on exit from the national territory, for any traveler carrying amounts exceeding the thresholds set by the legislation and regulations in force.

The following are required to declare in writing or electronically at the customs office:

- Any Algerian traveler (resident or non-resident) carrying amounts exceeding 1,000 euros or their equivalent in other currencies;

- Any foreign traveler carrying amounts exceeding 1,000 euros or their equivalent in other currencies.

The declaration obligation notably covers:
- Banknotes;
- Coins;
- All bearer payment instruments;
- Commercial paper;
- Securities and other negotiable instruments payable to bearer or capable of endorsement;
- Precious metals;
- Precious stones.

## 2 - On entry into the national territory

Any traveler is authorized to import currency without any limit on amount, subject to satisfying the declaration obligation with the customs services.

## 3 - On exit from the national territory

Resident and non-resident travelers are authorized to export:

- a maximum amount in cash equivalent to 7,500 euros, or its equivalent in another foreign currency, per calendar year, subject to the presentation of a bank debit notice for any withdrawal exceeding the regulatory thresholds made from a foreign currency account opened in Algeria;

- any amount covered by an exchange authorization issued by the Bank of Algeria;

- international bank cards;

- banknotes, coins, bearer payment instruments, commercial paper, securities and other negotiable and transferable instruments.

Non-resident travelers are required to present a bank receipt attesting to the exchange of part or all of the currencies (exceeding 1,000 euros) declared upon entry into the national territory.

## 4 - Declaration procedure

The currency declaration may be made:

- electronically, before arrival at the customs office;
- in writing, on entry or exit from the national territory, in accordance with the regulatory forms.

Declaration forms are available at customs offices.

The declaration form is also downloadable from the official website of the General Directorate of Customs.

### Entry and exit of the Algerian dinar

Any resident traveler is authorized to import and export Algerian banknotes up to the limit of 10,000 Algerian dinars (10,000 DA).

## VERY IMPORTANT

- The declaration of currency, precious metals and precious stones is mandatory, as is that of coins, bearer payment instruments, commercial paper, securities and other negotiable instruments.

- The declaration is primarily submitted electronically before arrival at the customs office.

- It may also be made in writing in accordance with the regulatory forms.

- The declaration form is valid for a single stay only.

- Any declared amount must be presented to customs officers for control purposes.

- The shipment of foreign currency banknotes, coins or any bearer payment instrument by freight or mail is prohibited.

- The declaration must be made before crossing the limits of the places designated for customs control.

- Import and export operations of precious metals and precious stones are subject to the legislative and regulatory provisions in force.
